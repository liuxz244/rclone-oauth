Privacy Policy

This OAuth application is used exclusively by its owner to access their own Google Drive through rclone.

Google Drive data is accessed for personal file management, synchronization, and mounting on Linux.

OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone. No separate application server collects these credentials.

Google Drive data is not sold or shared with third parties by this personal integration.

Access can be revoked through Google Account settings.
